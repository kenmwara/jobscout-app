THE PULSE, FRAME BY FRAME · 420ms ON --spring-arrive

What it is. scale(1) → 1.05 at 38% → 1 on --spring-arrive, 420ms, 1 iteration. It fires when the card settles — after the eighth dot lands — so it reads as the verdict arriving rather than the page loading. 5% is deliberately small: at the pill's 78×22px that is about 4px of travel at the widest point. What it is not: it does not repeat, it does not fire on hover or on scroll, and it never fires below 80. A NEAR-MISS card runs the identical rose fill and then simply stops — no rule exists for it, so there is nothing to disable. Why the asymmetry: law 12 says never claim what the reader has not done. A celebration that fires on every result is not a signal, and celebrating a 32 would be the interface congratulating someone on a bad morning.
