This is a mockup of the native screens, driven for real. Paste a resume or upload one — the clip posts the file to the app's own /api/extract, which reads PDF, DOCX, TXT and MD — then tap the arrow: it posts to the same /api/score the apps use, so the fits, the verdicts and the evidence are Claude's. Until you do, the cards carry example scores computed from the posting id, and they say so. The postings are the live sweep. Tapping a card opens the application screen, the heart saves, and the market, theme and platform controls drive the same tokens the shipped apps use — it loads site/base.css directly, so it cannot drift from production. The hourly scoring limit is shared with the real site.
