180 — GATES

Why those, and not the rest

Before Claude sees anything, three deterministic checks read every posting from the sweep. They cost nothing, run in an instant, and each rejection carries its reason.

Remote-listed

The posting itself has to say remote — not "flexible", not "hybrid-friendly".

Region-eligible

Work authorization, checked against where the role can legally be done.

Title in scope

The title has to sit in the bands the candidate is searching. Cheapest gate, biggest cut.
