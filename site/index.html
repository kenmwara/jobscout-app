How JobScout works

Three steps, and the middle one is the only one that costs anything.

Resume · read in the browserread

Your resume, not a form

Upload or paste it. It is read in the browser, scored, and never stored — no account, no profile to keep current.

the day, narrowedbefore a paid call

A gate before the model

A deterministic filter drops most of the day before a single paid call. What survives is what could plausibly fit.

Fit 0–100 · strongest & weakestscored

A number and a reason

Every survivor gets a fit from 0 to 100 and a verdict in plain words. The top match gets a cover letter grounded in your resume alone.

Most job boards show you everything and call it choice. This one reads what you wrote, throws away the share that cannot fit, and tells you why about the rest.
